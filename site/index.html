Engineered for Purity and Speed

        
Combining the reliability of Rust with the symbolic power of Lisp.

      


      

        

          
⚡

          
Row-as-Stack Minimalist UI

          
Each terminal row is an independent stack of cards. Words are cards, styled with semantic ANSI colors. ASCII progress bars track crafting in real-time.

        


        

          
📐

          
CAD-Style Command Primitives

          
Inspired by AutoCAD: s 1 2 to stack, u 2 to unstack, m 3 10 to move, b to buy. Full history navigation and line editing.

        


        

          
🔮

          
Steel Scheme Rules Engine

          
100% of game rules, multiset pattern matching, and state live inside Steel Scheme. Direct Lisp God Mode: type (spawn-card! "Apple") anytime!

        


        

          
🖥️

          
Headless Virtual Buffer

          
Decoupled rendering layer. The UI renders to an in-memory 2D cell grid, enabling zero-headless testing, CI snapshot tests, and multi-platform output.

        


        

          
🌾

          
Survival Moon Cycle

          
Every 30 seconds, a new Moon begins. Feed your villagers with harvested berries and cooked food, or watch them starve into bone cards!

        


        

          
🧩

          
Effortless Modding

          
Write new recipes and cards in pure S-expressions. Add furnaces, weapons, or dungeon exploration without recompiling the host executable.

⌨️ CAD Command Cheat Sheet

          

            
              
CommandParametersDescription

            
            
              
s<from> <to>Stack cards from slot A onto slot B

              
u<slot>Unstack top card into a new slot

              
m<from> <to>Move stack to empty slot

              
b<pack>Buy card pack (basic, food)

              
o<slot>Open booster pack at slot

              
x<slot>Sell top card for gold

              
w[seconds]Fast forward time

              
p—Toggle pause / resume

              
(...)(Scheme code)Execute arbitrary Lisp expression
